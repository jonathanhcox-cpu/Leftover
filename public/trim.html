TRIM OFFCUT PLANNING

Use leftover trim by matching individual lengths to the job

Enter the total run for a quick estimate or add a cut list when you know the individual pieces required. Leftover checks full trim lengths and measured offcuts against those cuts and can account for saw kerf. That matters because several short remnants may add up to enough total footage while still failing to supply a single long casing or baseboard piece.

Match profile, thickness, species and finish before combining old and new trim. Mitered corners, scarf joints, damaged ends and pattern alignment can increase waste, so keep a reasonable reserve and verify each visible run before cutting. Use the result to identify the purchase gap and save any useful remainder. See the door and window trim guide and room trim guide.

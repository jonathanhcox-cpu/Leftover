BEFORE YOU CUT

Leftover answers the question before the store run.

Does Leftover guarantee every scrap will fit?

No. The calculator is a planning check. Total usable material can be sufficient while a particular scrap is the wrong shape for a required cut. Verify the real layout before cutting.

Why include a planning reserve?

Projects commonly lose usable material to cuts, defects, breakage, pattern matching and installation constraints. The reserve gives you a more realistic first estimate.

Are my measurements uploaded?

No. The current calculators run in your browser and do not require an account or photo upload.

What should I count as a usable leftover?

Only count material you would actually install: correct type and dimensions, undamaged, and large enough for a realistic project cut.

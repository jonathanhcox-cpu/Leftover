Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Browser-based calculations

The current calculators run in your browser without an account. We do not ask you to upload project photos or files.

Contact emails

If you email support@buildwithleftovers.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and investigate reported issues. Contact messages are handled through Google Workspace and your email provider. Please do not include sensitive information.

Local storage

Saved inventory and project plans may use browser localStorage. That data remains in the browser unless you clear site data or a feature explicitly states otherwise.

Google AdSense and advertising

Leftover may use Google AdSense. Google and advertising partners may use cookies, web storage, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, limit, personalize and measure ads, detect fraud and report performance.

Consent and regional requirements

Where law requires consent before advertising or storage technologies are used, advertising should follow the consent choices available for that region. Some regions require a certified consent-management platform before personalized ads can be served.

QR leftover labels

If you create a QR leftover label, Leftover encodes the material type, quantity and dimensions in the URL fragment after the # symbol. That fragment is processed in your browser and is not included in the page request sent to the web server. Anyone who has the printed QR label can read the encoded material details, so treat labels as visible household/workshop information.

Technical information

Hosting, network and advertising requests can transmit IP address, browser and device information, referring page, requested URL and timestamps as needed to deliver and secure the service.

Your privacy choices

You can clear saved Leftover data with site controls or browser site-data controls, and manage cookies in your browser. Advertising choices may also be available through Google and other providers.

Children

Leftover is a general-audience DIY utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Third parties and changes

Third-party services operate under their own privacy practices. This policy may be updated as the site changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.

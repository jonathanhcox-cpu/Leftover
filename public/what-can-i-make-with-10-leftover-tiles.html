TILE · QUANTITY-FIRST PROJECT IDEAS

What can I make with 10 leftover tiles?

If they are 12×12-inch tiles, ten full tiles contain 10 square feet of face area. That can be enough for a compact backsplash section, accent panel, niche, tabletop surface or small repair—but the exact tile dimensions and required cuts decide whether a project really fits.

PROJECT-SIZE CHECKS

What 10 full 12×12 tiles can potentially cover

6

Square-foot accent

A 3 × 2 foot niche or accent panel uses 6 square feet before cuts and reserve, leaving some material for trimming or spares.

8

Square-foot backsplash section

A 4 × 2 foot rectangular section uses 8 square feet before outlets, edge cuts and layout losses.

10

Square-foot theoretical maximum

Exactly 10 square feet leaves no allowance for breakage, layout cuts or spare matching tile.

DON'T USE AREA ALONE

The shape of the project can matter as much as the total area

Ten square feet of tile cannot automatically cover every 10-square-foot shape. A narrow strip, centered pattern, diagonal layout, outlet-heavy backsplash or irregular repair may require more pieces because the cut remnants cannot always be reused.

Use the tile calculator for the exact project dimensions and measured scraps. If your tiles are not 12×12, enter their real width and length rather than relying on this 10-square-foot example.

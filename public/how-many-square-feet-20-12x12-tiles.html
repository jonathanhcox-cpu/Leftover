How many square feet do 20 12×12 tiles cover?

20 tiles measuring 12×12 inches cover exactly 20 square feet of face area. Each tile is one square foot, so the calculation is 20 tiles × 1 ft² = 20 ft² before cuts, breakage or a spare-tile reserve.

THE FORMULA

20 tiles × 1 square foot each = 20 square feet

A 12-inch by 12-inch tile contains 144 square inches. One square foot also contains 144 square inches. For full 12×12 tiles, the number of tiles therefore equals the face-area square footage: 10 tiles = 10 ft², 20 tiles = 20 ft², and 30 tiles = 30 ft².

ROOM-SIZE EXAMPLES

What size area could 20 12×12 tiles cover?

On pure area, 20 square feet could equal a 4 × 5-foot rectangle, a 2 × 10-foot strip, or any other shape totaling 20 square feet. Real tile layouts rarely use every square inch perfectly, however. Perimeter cuts, obstacles, centered patterns and unusable partial tiles can increase the required quantity.

If your measured area is exactly 20 square feet, owning exactly 20 tiles gives you a zero-tile reserve. For a leftover repair or small project, the safest check is to enter the actual dimensions and tile quantity in the tile calculator rather than relying only on area.

WASTE & RESERVE

When 20 square feet of tile is not enough for 20 square feet of surface

A simple straight layout in a favorable room shape may have relatively little cutting loss. Diagonal layouts, niches, corners, plumbing penetrations or many narrow edge pieces can create more waste. Breakage also matters when you have a fixed pile of discontinued leftover tile that cannot easily be replaced.

If matching tile is difficult to obtain, consider keeping at least one usable piece uninstalled for a future repair when the project allows it. Leftover's calculator is designed for this exact situation: start with the quantity you already own and see whether the planned area leaves a shortage or surplus.

COMMON QUESTIONS

20-tile coverage FAQ

01

Are 20 12×12 tiles 20 square feet?

Yes. Each full 12×12-inch tile is exactly one square foot of face area, so twenty tiles equal twenty square feet before cuts and waste.

02

Are 20 tiles enough for a 20 ft² floor?

Not automatically. That leaves no allowance for perimeter cuts, breakage, layout losses or a spare matching tile.

03

Does grout change the calculation?

Grout joints affect the installed layout dimensions, but for purchasing or inventory planning it is safer to calculate from the actual tile dimensions and verify the full layout rather than treating grout as extra usable tile.

QUICK REFERENCE

What a plywood cut plan needs to account for

48×96

Standard sheet size

A nominal 4×8 sheet measures 48 by 96 inches before any edge trimming you choose to make.

32 ft²

Area per full sheet

Useful for a rough material estimate, but area alone cannot prove that all required parts will fit.

Kerf

Material lost to cuts

Each saw pass removes material. Tight cut plans should use the actual kerf of the blade being used.

CUT LIST METHOD

Start with the largest plywood parts, then use the offcuts

List every required part by finished width, finished length and quantity. Place the largest or most restrictive parts first because they have the fewest possible locations. Then fit smaller pieces into the remaining spaces. If a visible veneer has a grain direction, keep that orientation consistent rather than rotating a part simply to make it fit.

Measure leftover plywood as actual usable rectangles. A 20 × 30-inch offcut is much more informative than calling it “about four square feet.” Damage, screw holes, delamination and rough edges can shrink the usable rectangle, so measure inside those defects.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Why 32 square feet of parts can require more than one 4×8 sheet

Suppose the total area of a cut list is close to 32 square feet. That does not guarantee a one-sheet layout. Long shelves, wide cabinet sides or grain-sensitive panels can leave narrow unused strips that contain enough area on paper but cannot accept the remaining rectangles. This is why Leftover checks physical fit rather than using area alone.

Before buying another full sheet, enter usable scraps in the calculator above. A single appropriately sized offcut can sometimes absorb the awkward part that would otherwise force an additional sheet. COMMON QUESTIONS

Plywood cut calculator FAQ

01

How many 4×8 sheets do I need?

A full 4×8 sheet contains 32 square feet, but the final sheet count depends on whether every required rectangle can be arranged after kerf, edge trimming and grain constraints.

02

How much does saw kerf matter?

Kerf is the strip removed by the blade on every cut. Repeated cuts can consume enough width to make a tight layout fail, so use the actual blade kerf for precision work.

03

Can plywood scraps replace a full sheet?

Yes. Measure each offcut and match it to a required part. It also needs the correct thickness, grade, condition and face or grain orientation.

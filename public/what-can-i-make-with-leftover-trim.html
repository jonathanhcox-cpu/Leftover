What can I make with leftover trim?

Short pieces of baseboard, casing and decorative molding are useful when their actual lengths match the job. Start with the offcuts you have, not a generic project list.

PROJECTS BY OFFCUT LENGTH

Good uses for trim scraps

01

Small repairs

Save matching profile for damaged baseboard, door casing or window casing. A short exact-match piece can be more valuable than several feet of mismatched new trim.

02

Frames and decorative panels

Four compatible pieces can form a small picture frame, wall molding panel or decorative border. Measure every side and allow for miter cuts before committing the stock.

03

Returns and short runs

Small offcuts may fit closet returns, cabinet-side runs, transition details and other places where a full stock length would create unnecessary waste.

FIT MATTERS

Total linear feet can be misleading

Five short pieces may add up to eight feet but still cannot replace one continuous eight-foot run. Profile, thickness, finish, damage and joint placement matter too. Enter your required cuts and the pieces you own in the leftover trim calculator to check whether the offcuts actually fit.

For a whole-room estimate, use the baseboard and trim calculator. It helps translate perimeter and openings into linear footage and stock-length planning.

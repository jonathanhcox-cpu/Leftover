DIY SCRAP PROJECT IDEAS

A project idea generator built around leftover material you already own

Searching for what to make with leftover wood, plywood scraps, extra tile or flooring usually produces generic idea lists. Leftover starts with your actual material instead. Enter the quantity and dimensions you have and the project finder checks that stock against practical project recipes before ranking the closest matches.

The match is more useful than a simple square-foot comparison because rectangular materials are checked against real piece dimensions, and lumber or trim ideas are checked against individual cut lengths. Results are still estimates: defects, saw kerf, pattern direction, fasteners, structural requirements and installation rules can change what is actually usable.

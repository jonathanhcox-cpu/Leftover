FLOORING · EXACT QUANTITY ANSWER

How many square feet do 10 flooring planks cover?

If each plank is 7.5 × 48 inches, ten full planks contain 25 square feet of face area. One plank covers 2.5 square feet, so 10 × 2.5 = 25 square feet before cuts and reserve.

THE MATH

10 planks × 2.5 square feet each = 25 square feet

A 7.5 × 48-inch plank contains 360 square inches. Divide by 144 square inches per square foot and each plank represents 2.5 square feet.

REAL FLOOR CHECK

Twenty-five square feet of plank area does not guarantee a 25-square-foot room will fit

Row staggering, damaged locking edges, direction changes, minimum end-piece lengths and unusable offcuts can increase the actual number of planks needed. Matching product and finish also matter for repairs.

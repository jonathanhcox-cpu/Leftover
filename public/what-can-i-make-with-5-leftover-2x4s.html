2×4 LUMBER · QUANTITY-FIRST PROJECT IDEAS

What can I make with 5 leftover 2×4s?

If they are five full 8-foot boards, you have 40 raw linear feet of stock before saw kerf, defects and end cleanup. That is enough material for several small frame-style projects, but the actual cut list decides what fits.

PROJECT-SIZE CHECKS

What five full 8-foot 2×4s can potentially support

16

Feet of planter-frame cuts

Eight 2-foot frame pieces total 16 finished feet, leaving substantial stock for other parts before kerf.

14

Feet of bench-frame cuts

Two 4-foot rails plus four 1.5-foot supports total 14 finished feet and fit comfortably within five 8-foot boards.

40

Raw linear feet maximum

Five 8-foot boards contain 40 raw feet, but no cut can be longer than an individual board.

WHY THE CUT LIST MATTERS

Forty feet of lumber does not mean every 40-foot cut list fits

Board-by-board allocation matters. Long rails, repeated cuts and saw kerf can force an additional board even when the total footage appears sufficient.

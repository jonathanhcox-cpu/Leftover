FLOORING · PROJECT IDEAS BY PLANK SIZE

What can I make with leftover flooring?

Saved flooring is most valuable when the plank size, locking profile, finish and condition match a real repair or small space. Start with the exact pieces you own rather than treating every remnant as interchangeable square footage.

Useful flooring leftovers by type of stock

01

Full matching planks

Best candidates for localized floor repairs, closets, pantry floors and other small areas where the original product is still compatible.

02

Long partial planks

These may work at row ends, transitions or narrow repair areas when the locking edges and stagger rules permit reuse.

03

Short offcuts

Short scraps can be useful for test fitting, color matching or non-floor decorative projects, but may not satisfy minimum end-length or locking requirements.

FIT BEFORE AREA

Square footage alone can overstate usable flooring

A pile may contain enough total area for a closet but still fail because the usable planks are too short, have damaged locking edges or cannot maintain the required stagger pattern.
